Vanful

Pack it tight. Route it right.

A 3-minute delivery puzzle. Pack odd-shaped parcels into your van, plan the fastest route through town, and deliver before the shift clock runs out.

  1. Drag parcels into the van. Tap one to rotate.
  2. The door is at the back, so load the first stop by the door.
  3. Tap the houses in order, avoid red traffic, and beat the clock.
