# Positioning v2

**One line:** Vanful is the delivery puzzle where *how* you pack decides *how fast* you deliver.

**For:** casual puzzle players who like packing games (block-fitting, "pack the truck")
and want something they can finish on a coffee break, on phone or laptop, with no install.

**The gap it owns** (from `launch/competitors.md`): packing games ignore where the
parcels go, and routing games ignore how they fit. Vanful joins them with one rule:
**the door is at the back**. The first stop has to be loaded last, or you lose time
digging parcels out.

**Proof we can honestly show today** (no players yet):
- Instant play in a browser, no download or account; a 3-minute shift.
- A guided first delivery that teaches the door rule in under a minute (automated playthrough: ~7 s).
- Works with mouse, touch and keyboard.

**What we don't claim yet:** player counts, ratings, "fun" quotes, or awards. These come from the first real playtest.

## Message hierarchy
1. Hook: *Pack it tight. Route it right.*
2. The twist: *The door's at the back. Load the first stop last.*
3. The promise: *3 minutes, free, in your browser.*
