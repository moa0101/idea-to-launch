# Launch kit (drafts: nothing is posted without the user's yes)

Order matters: run the **real playtest first**. The board's condition is 5 people, one
shift each, measuring "played again?" and "understood the door rule untold?". The posts
below get stronger once they can say something true from that.

## 0. Playtest invite (send to 5 friends or colleagues)

> I made a tiny delivery puzzle game, about 3 minutes in a browser. Could you play one
> shift and tell me two things: did you press "Play again", and did the "door at the
> back" rule make sense without me explaining? Link: https://vanful.vercel.app Thank you!

Collect the answers in `launch/levels/v2-playtest.md`. The local `?stats` log on each tester's
device shows whether they finished the tutorial and how many shifts they played.

## 1. itch.io devlog (after uploading)

**Title:** Vanful: the door's at the back

> I wanted a packing puzzle where the packing *means* something. In Vanful the
> van's door is at the back, so the parcels for your first stop have to go in last.
> Pack them wrong and you lose seconds digging them out. Then you pick a route
> around jammed streets, and a fast route only pays off if the load order matches it.
>
> It's free, runs in the browser and takes 3 minutes. It also has a guided first
> delivery. I'd love to hear where it gets confusing.
