# Lifecycle emails (copy only, nothing is sent)

Sent only to people who joined the waitlist from the game. Every email ends with the
unsubscribe line. Plain text, in the brand voice (cheerful, snappy, fair).

## 1. Welcome (right after sign-up)

**Subject:** You're on the Vanful list 📦
**Preview:** One new mode, about once a month. That's it.

> Thanks for joining! You'll hear from us when there's something new to play: new modes,
> new vans and new towns. About once a month, never more.
>
> Today's Daily Load is waiting: same parcels for everyone, one scored run.
> Can you keep the door clear? → {{url}}
>
> Pack it tight. Route it right.
> The Vanful team
>
> *You're getting this because you signed up in Vanful. [Unsubscribe]({{unsubscribe}})*

> Your streak is {{streak}} days. Keep it going (Night Shift paint unlocks at 3).

**Rules:** no fake urgency, no invented numbers, one link that matters per email.
