# Board verdict (lean, one pass — lenses from founder-board/lenses.md)

Lenses are summaries of published frameworks, not the authors' views.

| lens | score | vote | top risks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offers | 6/10 | FUND IF | 1) casual web games monetise poorly — the market is huge but buyers spend little; 2) the dream outcome ("satisfying perfect pack") must land in the first 20 seconds or players leave |
| Monopoly | 5/10 | FUND IF | 1) many free packing jam games exist, no moat in the mechanic alone; 2) distribution: needs a portal (itch.io, CrazyGames, Poki) or virality (shareable score) |
| Product | 7/10 | FUND | 1) two mechanics (pack + route) can feel like two half-games — must be one decision; 2) drag-and-rotate on phones is fiddly if hit areas are small |

**Combined:** 2 FUND IF, 1 FUND — average 6.0. Build it as a free MVP to test fun.

## Conditions (checklist for the build)

- [ ] Packing order must matter for the route: **parcels for the first stop must be
      reachable at the van door**, so the two halves are one decision (Product + Monopoly).
- [ ] First playable action within 5 seconds of opening; tutorial is the first round itself.
- [ ] Big, forgiving touch targets; rotate by tap, snap to grid.
- [ ] Shareable end-of-shift score line (copy to clipboard) for distribution.
- [ ] Real test after build: 5 people play one shift; measure "played again?" (from launch plan).

**Strongest version the board sees:** "the Tetris-of-delivery that rewards planning":
a short shift where clever loading order makes the route faster.
