## The gap

Packing games ignore *where* the parcels go, and routing games ignore *how* they fit;
the one title found that combines both (Parcel Dash) is a paid, installed arcade game.
**A free, instant-play browser puzzle where packing order and route are one decision —
load the last stop first, pack tight, then drive the fastest path — is open.**

## Threat

Jam-sized games are easy to copy; the defence is feel (juice, satisfying snap) and the
pack-order↔route link, not technology.
