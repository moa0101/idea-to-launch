# Idea

A browser puzzle-arcade game about parcel delivery. Each round you get a queue of
oddly-shaped parcels and a van with a fixed-size cargo grid: pack as many as you can
(drag, rotate), then plan the delivery route across a small city map. Traffic and road
length make some routes faster. Packed parcels that reach their door score; a tight
pack plus a smart route scores big. A shift lasts 2–3 minutes.

- **Platform:** game-web (zero install, shareable link, works on phone and desktop)
- **Audience:** casual puzzle players (fans of packing / spatial puzzles and route
  planning), playing in short breaks on phone or laptop
- **Core action:** pack the van tight, then pick the fastest route to deliver
- **Monetisation:** free MVP (no ads, no payments)
- **Accounts:** none (single-player, local high score)
- **Origin:** user's seed "one-tap dodge game" was replaced at intake by "package
  delivery game: fit more boxes into fixed-size vehicles and deliver faster via different paths".
