# v3: Credible → Sellable

**User's step:** "deploy + Sellable". v2 goes to a Vercel URL for the 5-person playtest,
while v3 builds the Sellable layer.
**Challenger's note:** pricing before players is a guess. The plan builds the *mechanisms*
(return loop, capture, supporter path) and leaves the price to be tested with real players.

**Headline:** *The Daily Load: one shared shift a day, a streak, and a score worth sharing.*

| # | track | item | acceptance check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Product | Daily Load: date-seeded shift (same for everyone, UTC), one scored run per day, streak counter, "Daily #N" on the title | Two runs on the same date get identical rounds; after a scored daily the button shows done + score; the streak increments on consecutive days |
| 2 | Market/Sell | Share card: spoiler-free text (Daily #, score, one emoji per round: 🟩 door clear / 🟨 blocked / ⬜ undelivered, link) | Copy produces that text, with no layout spoilers |
| 3 | Sell | Garage: 3 van paints. Classic is free, Night Shift unlocks at a 3-day streak, Sunset needs the supporter code. Selected paint is used in the game | Locked paints can't be picked; the code unlocks Sunset; the choice persists |
| 4 | Sell | Email capture: "Get new modes by email" with an explicit consent line; posts to a `waitlist` table (Supabase REST, insert-only RLS); hidden unless configured | With a configured endpoint the form posts `{email, source}`; invalid emails are rejected; without config nothing is shown |
| 5 | Sell | `go-to-market/pricing.md`: model (free + supporter + portal ads), tiers, unit economics as formulas, anchors | Every number is cited or labelled as an assumption |
| 6 | Sell | `go-to-market/emails.md`: welcome, daily reminder (opt-in), new-mode announcement | Under 120 words each, with unsubscribe line |

**Not in this level:** real payments (itch.io handles them; publishing is your step), the CrazyGames ad SDK
(only needed at their Full Launch, after 500 plays), accounts, leaderboards, pitch deck.
